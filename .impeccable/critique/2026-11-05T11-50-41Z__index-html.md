GlanceVNC has a credible foundation, but the site explains its features more effectively than it demonstrates its value. The biggest opportunity is to show an actual remote desktop session and explain connection requirements before the paid-download decision.

## Design specificity

The product content is specific; the visual composition is only partly specific. Real iPad imagery, direct connections, Apple Pencil and precise compatibility caveats ground it in GlanceVNC. The glass navigation, pink/blue gradients and numbered cards could serve many Apple utility apps. Preserve the product honesty and give the composition more product evidence.

## Strengths

- Honest boundaries: paid purchase, no subscription, direct connections, own VPN and compatible-server requirements are explained.
- Simple navigation and recognizable App Store badges make the primary action easy to find.
- A real product screenshot and a consistent palette establish credibility.

## Priority issues

1. **P1 — Explain purchase fit beside the first download button.** The hero promises “wherever you need it”; iOS/iPadOS 18+, a configured server and own-VPN requirements appear later (`index.html:105–124`). A newcomer can reach the paid download without understanding setup. Add a concise requirements line beside the badge and a direct setup-guide link. Preserve the detailed qualifications below.
2. **P2 — Show an active remote session.** The only screenshot shows Saved Connections (`index.html:115`), rather than a desktop being controlled. Use a genuine session image with a meaningful task and caption; keep the saved-connections screen as secondary evidence. Do not fabricate product UI or capabilities.
3. **P2 — Give features a hierarchy.** Ten numbered, paragraph-heavy cards (`index.html:136–146`) all receive equal emphasis, and release notes repeat several benefits. Group around three outcomes—connect, control, keep private—with short supporting details. The four-column desktop grid leaves two cards in its final row; mobile source stacks ten cards with a 15rem minimum height each.
4. **P2 — Make Setup Help start with a next step.** The destination contains 26 sequential FAQ cards with no category index (`support.html`). Add task/OS starting points and grouped anchors for setup, controls, security and troubleshooting. Promote the existing Mac mini/iPad walkthrough and label other platform coverage accurately.
5. **P2 — Strengthen navigation legibility and touch targets.** Desktop inspection finds pale labels on translucent gray; exact contrast was not measured. The mobile source sets navigation height to 2.25rem/36px (`styles.css` mobile `.nav-pill`), below the 44px comfort target. Increase target height and measure contrast across scroll positions before choosing a darker bar or text.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

The first screen is calm and reassuring. The middle becomes a feature catalogue with little new visual proof; the final panel repeats reassurance without fully resolving setup fit. This is informational scanning load, not ten competing action buttons. Keep the easy opening, introduce one compelling product demonstration, and answer “Will this work with my computer?” before the final download.
