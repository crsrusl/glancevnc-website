Privacy Policy

GlanceVNC connects to your desktop directly or through an SSH gateway you configure. The app does not require a GlanceVNC account, does not run a remote desktop relay, and does not collect analytics in the app.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What data does GlanceVNC collect?

The GlanceVNC app does not collect personal data for the developer. The app stores the information you enter for saved VNC and RDP connections locally on your device and can sync saved profile metadata through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.

What data is stored on the device?

Saved connection profiles may include a connection name, host or IP address, port, optional username, chosen protocol, operating system, sign-in method, VNC sharing preference, approved RDP certificate fingerprint, optional certificate security settings, creation date, and last connected date. These profiles are stored locally in the app's Application Support storage. Per-connection session preferences are also stored on this device.

Are images stored on the device?

GlanceVNC caches thumbnail previews of remote desktops on your device for saved connections. If you choose a custom dashboard background, the app stores a resized copy on your device. These images are not included in CloudKit profile sync or sent to the developer. Deleting a saved connection removes its cached thumbnail. Choosing Use Default Background in Settings removes the saved custom background.

What data syncs with iCloud?

Saved profile metadata may sync through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database. This can include the connection name, host, port, optional username, protocol, sign-in method, legacy VNC sharing preference, approved RDP certificate fingerprint, optional public certificate or fingerprint used for strict security, creation date, updated date, and last connected date. VNC and RDP passwords, the Operating System choice and per-connection session preferences are not included in synced profiles.

Previously shared SSH keys may still sync when iCloud sync is enabled. Manage sync under Settings → iCloud sync.

How are passwords secured?

VNC and RDP passwords are stored in Apple Keychain and are eligible to sync between your devices through iCloud Keychain when it is enabled. Passwords are not stored inside connection profile files or CloudKit profile records, and are not sent to the developer.

SSH passwords, trusted-server approvals and newly generated or imported SSH private keys stay on your device.

How does nearby discovery use the network?

GlanceVNC can browse the local network for Bonjour VNC services advertised as _rfb._tcp.. Discovery results are used in the app to help create a connection and are not sent to the developer.

How are VNC and RDP connections used?

When you connect directly, GlanceVNC opens a TCP connection to the host and port you configured. If you enable SSH tunnelling, the app connects to that host and port through your SSH gateway. The selected protocol sends desktop sign-in details to the configured desktop server. RDP uses TLS and checks server certificates; an approved certificate fingerprint is saved with the connection profile. Compatible VeNCrypt VNC servers can use optional certificate-verified TLS. Standard Mac Screen Sharing does not support this strict VNC option.

Does GlanceVNC send remote desktop content to the developer?

No. Remote desktop frames are displayed in the app during your remote session. GlanceVNC does not send your remote screen content, host list, usernames, or passwords to the developer.

Does the GlanceVNC app use tracking, analytics, or advertising?

No. The GlanceVNC app does not include third-party advertising, tracking SDKs, or in-app analytics collection.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete saved connections in the app. Deleting a saved connection removes its local profile, deletes the associated password from Keychain, and syncs the deletion marker through iCloud when profile sync is available.

Does this website use analytics?

This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand visits and how people use its pages. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information about website activity, such as page views, referring sites, and browser and device information. This website analytics is separate from the GlanceVNC app and does not collect your remote desktop credentials or remote desktop content.
