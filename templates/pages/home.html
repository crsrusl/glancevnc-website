Start with your computer.

GlanceVNC is the viewer. Your computer provides the desktop. Here’s what you need to connect them.

Your Mac, on your iPad.

Enable Mac Screen Sharing and set a VNC password. Start on the same trusted local network, then add your own VPN for access away from home.

Follow the Mac mini & iPad guide
GlanceVNCScreen Sharing

Use the separately configured VNC password, rather than your Mac account password. Standard Mac Screen Sharing does not support the optional certificate-verified VNC mode.

Your Windows desktop, with you.

Your PC needs a compatible, reachable RDP or VNC server. Add its address and connection details in GlanceVNC; RDP computers are added manually.

Read RDP connection instructions
GlanceVNCRDP / VNC server

Start on your local network. For access away from home, use your own VPN that provides a route to your computer. RDP gateways and Azure sign-in are not supported.

Your Linux machine, close at hand.

Run a compatible VNC or RDP server on your Linux computer or Raspberry Pi. Add its address and credentials, or discover compatible VNC services locally.

Read VNC connection instructions
GlanceVNCVNC / RDP server

Server compatibility matters. Optional certificate-verified VNC requires VeNCrypt 0.2 with an X.509 mode. Check the support page for authentication and encoding details.
